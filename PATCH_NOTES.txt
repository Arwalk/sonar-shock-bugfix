SONAR SHOCK — COMMUNITY BUGFIX PATCH (v1.2)
============================================
Applied to: Sonar Shock.pck (Godot 4.2.2). The original file is preserved
unmodified as "Sonar Shock.pck.orig" — restore it by deleting the patched
"Sonar Shock.pck" and renaming the .orig back. The exe, Steam DLLs, and your
save files are untouched, and existing saves remain fully compatible.

WHAT WAS FIXED
--------------
1. Stuck under objects after crouching (movement)
   - Standing up was gated by a single zero-width ray at the player's center,
     so standing under the EDGE of a shelf/table/duct passed the check while
     the growing body clipped the object, wedging the player permanently.
     Standing clearance is now a full-body capsule test.
   - The crouch animation shrank the collider with a non-uniform scale, which
     Godot physics does not support reliably, and regrew it over 0.2 s AFTER
     the check. The collider is now resized exactly and instantly at the
     moment the clearance check passes.
   - New safety net: if the player ever ends up embedded in geometry from
     above (closing doors, elevators, old "stuck" saves), the game now
     auto-ducks them free ("Forced to crouch.") within a fraction of a second.
   - Respawning while crouched no longer force-stands you into an obstacle.

2. Performance decay over long sessions (the "memory leak")
   - Bullet holes, shell casings, and blood were parented to the scene root
     and, with the default decal lifetime setting ("OFF" = keep forever),
     were never deleted — hit-blood sprites survived even level changes,
     deaths, and returning to the menu. After a few hours, thousands of live
     objects crushed the framerate until a full game restart.
   - Decals now register in a bounded registry: the newest 300 bullet holes,
     250 blood effects, and 150 casings are kept and the oldest are recycled.
     Level transitions now also clean up hit-blood correctly.
   - The decal lifetime option still works exactly as before; "OFF" now means
     "until recycled by the cap" instead of "leak forever".

3. Elevator softlock (low sanity)
   - The "crazy elevator ride" rolled a random destination with float
     arguments (randi_range(0.6, 5.4)), which could produce 0 — matching no
     destination, so the elevator closed its doors and did nothing. The roll
     is now a proper 1..5.

4. Stuck holding a barrel — "Not enough space." (new in v1.1)
   - Putting down a carried item was gated by a fixed test cube in front of
     the player that bore no relation to the item's size: while crouched its
     bottom sank below the floor, so the floor itself denied EVERY placement,
     and any wall, step, or nearby prop (e.g. the barrel clusters on floor 3)
     blocked it too. Barrels and soma mines couldn't be thrown away either
     (their throw repeated the same broken check), and weapons stay disabled
     while carrying — a soft-lock in tight rooms.
   - Placement is now checked with a volume shaped like the actual carried
     item, sitting where the item actually lands, ignoring flat floors and
     the player's own body. Throwing a barrel/mine now only requires open
     space directly ahead (like every other item), so you can always throw
     your way out. Dropping face-first into a wall is still denied, and the
     denial sound no longer machine-guns while the button is held.

5. Stuttering, worst on floor 3 (new in v1.2)
   - The floor-3 security-monitor screen plays a "video" made of 1,314
     separate image files at 45 fps, on a loop, from the moment you enter
     the floor — even through walls. It is by far the heaviest animation in
     the game (the runner-up is 31 frames) and caused recurring hitches all
     over the floor. It now pauses automatically whenever it is off screen
     and plays normally when you actually look at it.
   - Every enemy that had reached you made the engine print an internal
     error to the console EVERY physics frame (a failed "look_at" with a
     degenerate direction) — console writes mid-frame are a classic stutter
     source on Windows, and this fired exactly during melee combat. All 13
     such call sites across 11 enemy scripts are now guarded. Blood
     splatter landing on floors/ceilings triggered one more such error per
     hit; its decal aiming was fixed too (this also corrects subtly wrong
     splatter orientation on sloped surfaces).
   - The player's position was pushed to every living enemy's navigation
     agent 60x per second, forcing full path recomputation on every sample;
     it is now pushed ~7x per second (enemies read it on their own timers
     anyway — combat behavior is unchanged, pathfinding just stops thrashing).
   - Crossing into the floor-3 crypt/cyber area toggles lightmaps and fog,
     which can hitch at the doorway while shaders compile; those shader
     variants are now pre-warmed during the elevator ride onto the floor.
   - A low-sanity jumpscare printed two debug lines to the console
     mid-game; removed.

6. Smaller fixes
   - Level unloading now uses deferred deletion (queue_free) instead of
     freeing live maps mid-frame (crash risk).
   - bullethole4.gd had "speed_scale == randf_range(...)" (a comparison doing
     nothing) instead of an assignment — dragonbreath decal flicker variance
     restored.

HOW IT WAS VERIFIED
  A headless Godot 4.2.2 test harness mounts this exact pck, instantiates the
  real player scene over synthetic test geometry (including the exact
  edge-overhang case that triggered the stuck bug), simulates crouch input,
  and asserts 93 checks covering the crouch fix, the anti-stuck watchdog, the
  decal cap, the cleanup paths, item placement (standing and crouched
  drops, wall/blocked-volume denials, and the throw escape), and the
  stutter fixes (a live enemy ticking on a degenerate nav target with the
  test runner failing if a single look_at error reaches the console) — all
  passing. The decompiled sources,
  tools, and test suite are in the "work/" folder (git repository).
