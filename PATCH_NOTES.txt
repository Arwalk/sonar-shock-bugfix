SONAR SHOCK — COMMUNITY BUGFIX PATCH
=====================================
Applied to: Sonar Shock.pck (Godot 4.2.2). The original file is preserved
unmodified as "Sonar Shock.pck.orig" — restore it by deleting the patched
"Sonar Shock.pck" and renaming the .orig back. The exe, Steam DLLs, and your
save files are untouched, and existing saves remain fully compatible.

WHAT WAS FIXED
--------------
1. Stuck under objects after crouching (movement)
   - Standing up was gated by a single zero-width ray at the player's center,
     so standing under the EDGE of a shelf/table/duct passed the check while
     the growing body clipped the object, wedging the player permanently.
     Standing clearance is now a full-body capsule test.
   - The crouch animation shrank the collider with a non-uniform scale, which
     Godot physics does not support reliably, and regrew it over 0.2 s AFTER
     the check. The collider is now resized exactly and instantly at the
     moment the clearance check passes.
   - New safety net: if the player ever ends up embedded in geometry from
     above (closing doors, elevators, old "stuck" saves), the game now
     auto-ducks them free ("Forced to crouch.") within a fraction of a second.
   - Respawning while crouched no longer force-stands you into an obstacle.

2. Performance decay over long sessions (the "memory leak")
   - Bullet holes, shell casings, and blood were parented to the scene root
     and, with the default decal lifetime setting ("OFF" = keep forever),
     were never deleted — hit-blood sprites survived even level changes,
     deaths, and returning to the menu. After a few hours, thousands of live
     objects crushed the framerate until a full game restart.
   - Decals now register in a bounded registry: the newest 300 bullet holes,
     250 blood effects, and 150 casings are kept and the oldest are recycled.
     Level transitions now also clean up hit-blood correctly.
   - The decal lifetime option still works exactly as before; "OFF" now means
     "until recycled by the cap" instead of "leak forever".

3. Elevator softlock (low sanity)
   - The "crazy elevator ride" rolled a random destination with float
     arguments (randi_range(0.6, 5.4)), which could produce 0 — matching no
     destination, so the elevator closed its doors and did nothing. The roll
     is now a proper 1..5.

4. Smaller fixes
   - Level unloading now uses deferred deletion (queue_free) instead of
     freeing live maps mid-frame (crash risk).
   - bullethole4.gd had "speed_scale == randf_range(...)" (a comparison doing
     nothing) instead of an assignment — dragonbreath decal flicker variance
     restored.
